Your own soundtrack for Sovereign Command

Put music files (mp3, ogg, wav, m4a, flac...) in this folder and list them in
playlist.json, in the order they should play:

[
  { "title": "Main theme", "file": "main-theme.mp3" },
  { "title": "War room", "file": "war-room.ogg" }
]

They appear under MUSIC FOLDER in the in-game music player (the note icon in
the top bar), next to the game's own soundtrack. Players can also add files of
their own from the player ("Add music files..."); those are kept on their device.
Only include music you have the rights to share.
